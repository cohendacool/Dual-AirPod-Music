Import Music from Your Computer

USB transfers copy music files onto your phone first. A website cannot directly browse your computer over USB.

Android phone + Windows PC

  1. Connect your phone to the computer with a USB cable.
  2. Unlock the phone and choose File transfer or Transferring files in its USB notification.
  3. On the computer, open your phone in File Explorer and copy audio files into its Music folder.
  4. Return to this app on your phone and tap Choose Transferred Files.

iPhone

iPhones do not generally expose a normal Music folder to a website over USB. Transfer supported audio files into the Files app using a compatible Finder or file-sharing workflow, then select them here.

Computer browser

If you are using the app on the computer itself, you can simply tap Add Songs and choose the audio files directly.
